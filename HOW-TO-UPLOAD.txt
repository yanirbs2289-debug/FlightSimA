Yarpa A Simulator – update package

Contents:
  index.html + version.json            -> regular version
  test/index.html + test/version.json  -> test version

Publishing this update:
 1. Go to github.com and open your yarpa-updates repository.
 2. Click "Add file" > "Upload files".
 3. Drag everything in this folder (including the test folder) into the page. The new files replace the old ones.
 4. Click "Commit changes".
 Within a few minutes, everyone who opens the app gets the new version.

Check: open https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YOUR-USERNAME/yarpa-updates/main/version.json
and make sure it shows the new version number.

Do not edit index.html or version.json by hand – version.json contains a checksum and the app will reject a modified file.
